### What We Do

**Penetration Testing (PTaaS)**
Web app, API, cloud, mobile, desktop, network, Active Directory, AI agent, and red team engagements. Findings stream into your client portal in real time — no waiting weeks for a PDF.

**Lory AI Pentester**
Autonomous, always-on testing that runs alongside our human testers. New code and new exposures get tested as they emerge, not once a year.

**Incident Response & Defense**
IR retainers, ransomware and BEC response, digital forensics, tabletop exercises, threat hunting, and purple team.

**Compliance + vCISO**
SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, PCI DSS, HIPAA, CMMC, and more. Lory handles routing, evidence automation, and audit readiness.

**Code & Access Reviews**
Hardcoded secrets, missing auth, open APIs — caught before production. Includes dedicated reviews for AI-generated ("vibe coded") apps.

**Security Awareness Training**
Phishing simulations with real-time analytics and compliance-ready reporting.
